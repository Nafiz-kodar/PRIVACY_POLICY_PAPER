Privacy Policy

Home/Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2025

At koleer.com.bd, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you interact with our website, services, and physical store.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to process your orders and improve our services:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, billing and delivery address.

  • Payment Information: Transaction details (processed securely through our payment partners).

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and browsing activity on our website.

  • In-Store Data: Contact details shared for warranty, service, or purchase registration.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders.

  • Communicate order updates, offers, or product support.

  • Improve our website, products, and customer service.

  • Comply with legal obligations and fraud prevention.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to any third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Payment transactions are encrypted and handled through trusted third-party providers.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience. Cookies help us remember your preferences and track website performance. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect site functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools (such as payment gateways or delivery partners) that collect limited information necessary to perform their services. These parties are required to maintain data privacy standards.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal information.

  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.

  • Request details about how your data is used or shared.

To exercise these rights, please contact us via email.

7. Retention of Data

We retain your information only as long as necessary for business, legal, or accounting purposes.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on our website with a new “Last Updated” date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact:
