Privacy at AYESH

Your information. Handled with care.

This policy explains what information AYESH collects, how it is used and the choices available to you.

Effective: 27 July 2026 Applies to ayesh.com.bd Bangladesh

Clear, practical and transparent.

We collect only the information needed to process orders, provide support and operate the website.

What we collect

Name, phone, address, order and basic website-use data.

Why we use it

To process orders, provide support and improve the website.

Who may receive it

Courier, payment and technical providers needed to serve you.

Your choices

You may request correction, deletion or marketing opt-out.

01

Information we collect

Only information needed to serve you.

We may collect your name, phone number, email, delivery address, order details, messages, account details and basic device or cookie data.

02

How we use it

For orders, support, security and website operation.

  • Process, confirm, deliver, track or resolve orders.
  • Provide customer support and order updates.
  • Protect the website and prevent misuse or fraud.
  • Improve products, website performance and service quality.
  • Send promotions where permitted, with an unsubscribe option.
03

Payments and delivery

Necessary information may be shared to complete your order.

We may share order details with courier, payment, banking, mobile-financial-service and technical providers. Payment credentials are handled by the relevant payment provider.

AYESH does not sell customer personal information.
04

Cookies

Cookies support cart, checkout, login and site performance.

You may block or delete cookies through your browser, but some website features may stop working correctly.

05

Service providers

We share only what is necessary.

Information may be shared with hosting, courier, payment, analytics, communication, security and support providers, or where required by law.

06

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed.

Order, payment-status, delivery and support records may be retained for business, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention or legal purposes. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online system is completely secure.

07

Your choices

You may contact us about information linked to you.

We take practical steps to reduce unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or loss. However, no website, transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers should protect their account password, avoid sharing verification codes and contact AYESH if they suspect unauthorized activity.

Questions about your privacy or information?

Contact AYESH and include enough information for us to understand and review your request.
