Gazaria Popular Hospital and Diagnostic Centre is committed to protecting your personal information and privacy. This Privacy Policy explains the limited information we collect through our website, Facebook Page, phone, Messenger, WhatsApp, or direct contact channels, and how that information is used.

গজারিয়া পপুলার হসপিটাল ও ডায়াগনস্টিক সেন্টার আপনার ব্যক্তিগত তথ্যের গোপনীয়তা রক্ষায় প্রতিশ্রুতিবদ্ধ। অনলাইন চ্যানেলগুলো সাধারণ যোগাযোগ ও callback সমন্বয়ের জন্য; চিকিৎসা পরামর্শ, রিপোর্ট, উপসর্গ বা diagnosis পাঠানোর জন্য নয়।

1. Health Data Handling

Gazaria Popular Hospital is a rural healthcare facility. We do not store medical records, diagnostic reports, or patient health data online or in any cloud system. Our clinical workflow is entirely in-hospital and paper-based:

  • All medical reports, ultrasound results, and pathology reports are handed physically to the patient at the hospital.
  • We do not operate any Electronic Medical Record (EMR) system, patient portal, online health dashboard, or cloud storage of patient data.
  • Our public website pages do not collect report uploads, diagnostic inputs, medical history, symptoms, prescriptions, or test results.

আমরা অনলাইন বা ক্লাউডভিত্তিক কোনো মেডিকেল রেকর্ড, ডায়াগনস্টিক রিপোর্ট বা রোগীর স্বাস্থ্য তথ্য সংরক্ষণ করি না। সকল মেডিকেল রিপোর্ট রোগীকে সরাসরি হাতে হাতে প্রদান করা হয়।

1.1 Internal Hospital Records

For in-person hospital registration and coordination, authorized hospital staff may record limited administrative information in an internal, access-restricted (PIN-protected) system:

  • Patient name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Basic administrative date or area information when needed for registration
  • General visit coordination information recorded by staff inside the hospital workflow

This internal system is used solely for scheduling and registering the appointment itself. It does not store medical reports, diagnostic results, test data, ultrasound images, prescriptions, or clinical notes. All medical documentation remains on paper and is handed physically to the patient. Public visitors have no access to this staff-only system.

সরাসরি হাসপাতালে নিবন্ধন ও সমন্বয়ের জন্য অনুমোদিত কর্মীরা PIN-সুরক্ষিত অভ্যন্তরীণ সিস্টেমে সীমিত প্রশাসনিক তথ্য লিপিবদ্ধ করতে পারেন। কোনো মেডিকেল রিপোর্ট, ডায়াগনস্টিক ফলাফল, প্রেসক্রিপশন বা ক্লিনিক্যাল নোট এখানে সংরক্ষণ করা হয় না।

2. Scope of Online Contact

Information collected via our website, Facebook Page, Messenger, or WhatsApp is used solely for appointment coordination and general inquiries. These channels are not used for diagnosis, medical consultation, or delivery of reports. All medical discussions and reports take place in person at the hospital.

আমাদের ওয়েবসাইট, Facebook Page, Messenger বা WhatsApp-এর মাধ্যমে সংগ্রহ করা তথ্য শুধুমাত্র অ্যাপয়েন্টমেন্ট সমন্বয় ও সাধারণ যোগাযোগের জন্য ব্যবহৃত হয়।

3. Information We Collect

For general contact, callback coordination, hospital registration, website operation, analytics, and advertising measurement, we may collect the following limited information:

  • Name (নাম)
  • Mobile phone number (মোবাইল নম্বর)
  • Area or approximate location if needed for callback coordination
  • Communication source or channel, such as website, phone, Facebook Page, Messenger, WhatsApp, Call Button, or Meta Lead Form
  • General message or preferred callback time that you choose to provide
  • Basic technical and analytics data, such as IP address, browser/device information, approximate location derived from IP address, pages viewed, referring page, timestamps, cookie or device identifiers where applicable, and ad interaction data

We do not collect sensitive health information, medical history, diagnostic results, prescriptions, or test reports through this website or any online channel.

ওয়েবসাইট, Facebook, Messenger, WhatsApp বা Meta Lead Form-এ চিকিৎসা-সংক্রান্ত ব্যক্তিগত তথ্য, রিপোর্ট, উপসর্গ, diagnosis, prescription বা test result পাঠাবেন না। এসব বিষয় সরাসরি হাসপাতালে আলোচনা করা উচিত।

4. Purpose of Data Collection

The limited information above is used only for:

  • Appointment confirmation and follow-up communication
  • Patient registration (in the hospital's internal paper records)
  • Responding to your questions or appointment requests
  • Improving the quality of our service
  • Measuring website performance, understanding which pages are visited, and improving the website experience
  • Measuring advertising performance and understanding which ads or channels generate general enquiries
  • Meeting legal or administrative requirements

5. Analytics, Facebook, Messenger & Online Advertising

If you contact us through our Facebook Page, Messenger, WhatsApp, Call Button, or Meta Lead Form, we may collect your name, phone number, preferred callback time, area/location, and general message so our team can contact you and coordinate your enquiry.

Our Meta Lead Forms should be used only for general contact and callback requests. Please do not submit medical-service details, symptoms, reports, diagnosis, pregnancy-related information, prescription details, treatment details, or other sensitive health information through Meta Lead Forms, Messenger, WhatsApp, or website messages. These matters should be discussed directly with hospital staff in person or by phone.

We may use analytics and advertising technologies, such as Cloudflare Web Analytics, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Meta Conversions API, or similar tools, to measure website visits, improve the website, protect the site from misuse, understand ad performance, and build general marketing audiences where permitted.

These tools may collect or receive technical data such as page views, clicks, visit time, referring page, browser/device details, IP address, cookie or device identifiers where applicable, and ad interaction data. If we use Meta Pixel, Conversions API, custom audiences, or similar advertising tools, we will not intentionally send medical records, diagnostic results, prescriptions, clinical notes, symptoms, report data, detailed treatment information, or patient-level hospital transaction information to advertising platforms.

We do not sell your personal information. Where required by law or platform rules, we will use appropriate consent, notice, or opt-out controls before using cookies, pixels, or similar advertising technologies.

6. Medical Information

All medical discussion, diagnosis, consultation, and report delivery happen in person at the hospital. Information sent via online or social media channels is not considered a complete medical record. In case of a medical emergency, please contact your nearest hospital or emergency service directly.

7. Third-Party Sharing Policy

We do not sell, rent, or unnecessarily transfer your personal information to third parties. Limited sharing may occur:

  • With authorized doctors, staff, or administrative departments of the hospital (for providing the requested service)
  • With relevant authorities when required by law or government directive

8. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. Our paper-based record system is not exposed to online data breach risk. However, no internet-based communication (phone, Messenger) can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

  • Opt out of promotional communications
